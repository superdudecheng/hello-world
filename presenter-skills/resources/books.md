# 📚 Books

You don't need to read all of these. Pick one from "Start here," read it while
doing the program, and go deeper only where you want to.

## Start here (pick one)
- **_Talk Like TED_ — Carmine Gallo.** Reverse-engineers the most popular TED
  talks into 9 repeatable principles. The best single practical starting point.
- **_Confessions of a Public Speaker_ — Scott Berkun.** Honest, funny, and
  practical — written by someone who speaks for a living. Demystifies the whole
  thing.

## Content & structure
- **_Resonate_ and _Slide:ology_ — Nancy Duarte.** The definitive works on
  structuring a talk and designing visuals that serve it.
- **_Made to Stick_ — Chip & Dan Heath.** Why some ideas survive and others die.
  The "SUCCESs" framework (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional,
  Stories) will change how you build every talk.

## Storytelling
- **_Storyworthy_ — Matthew Dix.** The best practical book on finding and
  telling true personal stories. From a multi-time Moth GrandSLAM champion.
- **_The Storytelling Animal_ — Jonathan Gottschall.** Why humans are wired for
  narrative.

## Delivery, voice & presence
- **_Steal the Show_ — Michael Port.** Treating every talk as a performance.
- **_TED Talks: The Official TED Guide to Public Speaking_ — Chris Anderson.**
  From TED's curator. Broad, modern, and reliable.

## Rhetoric & persuasion (advanced)
- **_Thank You for Arguing_ — Jay Heinrichs.** The most enjoyable introduction to
  classical rhetoric (ethos/pathos/logos) you'll find.
- **_On Speaking Well_ — Peggy Noonan.** From a presidential speechwriter. On
  writing words meant to be *heard*, not read.

## Mindset & anxiety
- **_The Charisma Myth_ — Olivia Fox Cabane.** Charisma as a set of learnable
  behaviors, not an innate gift.
