# 🗓️ The 12-Week Program

A structured path from nervous beginner to confident, compelling speaker. It
assumes **~3–4 hours per week**: one curriculum module, a few videos, daily
drills, and — most importantly — *speaking out loud* every week.

> **Before you start:** schedule a real talk for around **Week 10–12**. A team
> demo, a meetup lightning talk, a toast, a lesson — anything with a live
> audience and a date. Everything below builds toward it. A deadline is the
> single most powerful learning accelerator you have.

Track everything in [`practice/progress-tracker.md`](practice/progress-tracker.md).

---

## Phase 1 — Foundations (Weeks 1–3)
*Goal: get comfortable being on your feet and saying something clear.*

### Week 1 — Mindset & baseline
- **Read:** [Foundations & mindset](curriculum/00-foundations.md)
- **Watch:** Julian Treasure, *"How to speak so that people want to listen"*
- **Do:** Record a **2-minute talk** on "something I know well." This is your
  **baseline** — save the video. You'll compare against it in Week 12.
- **Drill:** Daily vocal warm-ups (see [drills](practice/drills.md)).

### Week 2 — Content & structure
- **Read:** [Content & structure](curriculum/01-content-and-structure.md)
- **Watch:** Nancy Duarte, *"The secret structure of great talks"*
- **Do:** Take any topic and structure a **3-minute talk** using one clear
  message + three supporting points. Speak it aloud (don't write a script).

### Week 3 — Voice & delivery
- **Read:** [Voice & vocal delivery](curriculum/02-voice-and-delivery.md)
- **Watch:** Will Stephen, *"How to sound smart in your TEDx Talk"*
- **Do:** Re-record your Week 2 talk applying **pace, pause, and pitch**. Watch
  both versions back-to-back.

---

## Phase 2 — Delivery & presence (Weeks 4–6)
*Goal: make your body and voice reinforce your message.*

### Week 4 — Body language & stage presence
- **Read:** [Body language & stage presence](curriculum/03-body-language.md)
- **Watch:** A Toastmasters World Championship performance; Amy Cuddy on presence.
- **Do:** Record a talk and watch it **on mute.** Fix one habit (pacing, closed
  posture, hands in pockets, no eye line).

### Week 5 — Storytelling (part 1: finding stories)
- **Read:** [Storytelling](curriculum/04-storytelling.md)
- **Watch:** Brené Brown *"The power of vulnerability"*; one story from The Moth.
- **Do:** Write down **five true stories** from your life. Pick one and tell it in
  90 seconds, out loud, no notes.

### Week 6 — Storytelling (part 2: telling them well)
- **Re-read:** the storytelling module's structure section.
- **Watch:** Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie, *"The danger of a single story."*
- **Do:** Retell your Week 5 story with a clear **beginning → turn → resolution**
  and one vivid sensory detail. Record it.

---

## Phase 3 — Craft & polish (Weeks 7–9)
*Goal: add visuals, handle a room, and sharpen persuasion.*

### Week 7 — Slides & visual aids
- **Read:** [Slides & visual aids](curriculum/05-visuals-and-slides.md)
- **Watch:** Hans Rosling, *"The best stats you've ever seen"* (data done right).
- **Do:** Build a **5-slide deck** for a short talk. Rule: no slide has more than
  6 words or one image. Rehearse *talking to* the slides, not reading them.

### Week 8 — Reading the room & Q&A
- **Read:** [Reading the room & Q&A](curriculum/06-audience-and-qa.md)
- **Watch:** Matt Abrahams, *"Think Fast, Talk Smart"* (Stanford).
- **Do:** Have a friend fire **hard questions** at you on your topic. Practice the
  pause-then-answer technique. Record it.

### Week 9 — Rhetoric & persuasion
- **Read:** [Advanced rhetoric & persuasion](curriculum/08-advanced-rhetoric.md)
- **Watch:** Steve Jobs, Stanford 2005 Commencement; MLK, *"I Have a Dream."*
- **Do:** Rewrite your talk's opening and closing using **one rhetorical device**
  (rule of three, a rhetorical question, or a callback).

---

## Phase 4 — Performance (Weeks 10–12)
*Goal: deliver the real thing.*

### Week 10 — Build the real talk
- **Read:** [Nerves & performance mindset](curriculum/07-nerves-and-mindset.md)
- **Do:** Assemble your full talk for your scheduled event. First full rehearsal,
  recorded and timed.

### Week 11 — Rehearse to performance level
- **Do:** Rehearse the full talk **at least 3 times.** Once for a live human. Get
  feedback using the [rubric](practice/self-assessment-rubric.md). Refine.
- **Drill:** Nerves-management routine (breathing, warm-up, reframe).

### Week 12 — Deliver, then review
- **Do:** **Give the talk.** 🎉
- **Then:** Record a fresh **2-minute talk** on the same prompt as your Week 1
  baseline. Watch them side by side. Write down what changed in
  [`progress-tracker.md`](practice/progress-tracker.md).
- **Next:** Pick your next talk and next skill to deepen. This is a practice, not
  a finish line.

---

## Faster track (have a talk in 2 weeks?)

Do this instead, in order:
1. [Content & structure](curriculum/01-content-and-structure.md) — get the message right
2. [Storytelling](curriculum/04-storytelling.md) — add one story
3. [Voice & delivery](curriculum/02-voice-and-delivery.md) — pace and pause
4. [Nerves & performance mindset](curriculum/07-nerves-and-mindset.md) — calm the body
5. Rehearse out loud, on your feet, recorded, **at least 4 times.**

Skip the rest until after the talk. Structure + one good story + a calm body
will carry you further than any amount of slide polish.
