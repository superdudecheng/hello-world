# 🎤 The Expert Presenter Project

A self-guided project for becoming a confident, compelling public speaker and
orator — whether you're pitching to a boardroom, giving a conference keynote,
teaching, or speaking at a wedding.

This isn't a book to read once. It's a **practice program**. You improve at
speaking the same way you improve at an instrument: deliberate practice, honest
feedback, and repetition over weeks.

## The 9 core skills (the curriculum)

1. [Foundations & mindset](curriculum/00-foundations.md) — why we fear speaking, and how experts think
2. [Content & structure](curriculum/01-content-and-structure.md) — say something worth hearing, in the right order
3. [Voice & vocal delivery](curriculum/02-voice-and-delivery.md) — pace, pause, pitch, projection
4. [Body language & stage presence](curriculum/03-body-language.md) — gesture, posture, movement, eye contact
5. [Storytelling](curriculum/04-storytelling.md) — the single highest-leverage skill
6. [Slides & visual aids](curriculum/05-visuals-and-slides.md) — support the message, never compete with it
7. [Reading the room & Q&A](curriculum/06-audience-and-qa.md) — audience awareness and handling questions
8. [Nerves & performance mindset](curriculum/07-nerves-and-mindset.md) — turning anxiety into energy
9. [Advanced rhetoric & persuasion](curriculum/08-advanced-rhetoric.md) — the classical tools of great orators

## How to use this project

1. **Set a real deadline.** Nothing accelerates learning like a scheduled talk.
   Volunteer for one — a team demo, a meetup lightning talk, a toast. Put a date
   on it.
2. **Follow the [12-week plan](12-week-plan.md)** (or move faster if you have a
   talk coming up sooner).
3. **Record yourself constantly.** Your phone is your most important tool. You
   cannot fix what you cannot see.
4. **Do the drills.** See [`practice/drills.md`](practice/drills.md). Ten
   minutes a day beats a three-hour cram.
5. **Get feedback.** Join a group (see
   [`resources/tools-and-communities.md`](resources/tools-and-communities.md)).
   Real audiences beat solo practice.
6. **Track your progress** in [`practice/progress-tracker.md`](practice/progress-tracker.md).

## The one rule that matters most

> **You learn to speak by speaking, not by reading about speaking.**

Everything here is scaffolding around that truth. Watch the videos, absorb the
frameworks — but the improvement happens the moment you stand up and open your
mouth. Aim to be *on your feet* every single week of this program.
