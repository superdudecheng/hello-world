The 6-step method

You don't need to learn Mandarin — you need to erase the Cantonese accent tells and add native polish.

  • Step 0 — Baseline. Record yourself reading a paragraph today. Re-record monthly to hear progress (use the Progress Log tab).
  • Step 1 — Target accent. Aim for 标准普通话 (standard Putonghua), the target of the official 普通话水平测试 (PSC) exam — huge amount of free material.
  • Step 2 — Build your library. See the Resources tab for YouTube searches, apps, and sites.
  • Step 3 — Core method. Shadowing + Minimal pairs + Record-and-compare. These three do 90% of the work.
  • Step 4 — Weekly routine. ~30–45 min/day, each day targeting one accent tell (Routine tab).
  • Step 5 — Native flavor. Add fillers (就, 那个, 呗, 嘛), tone sandhi reflexes, and colloquialisms once your sounds are clean.
The core loop: Play a native clip → shadow it (speak simultaneously) → record yourself → play both back-to-back → hear the gap → repeat. That feedback loop is what rewires muscle memory.
